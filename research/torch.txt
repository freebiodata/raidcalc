Torches provide light. They are one of two things the player will spawn with in Experimental Rust (the other being the [[Rock]]). Right click to turn them off and on. Once it's on, a Torch will slowly burn fuel, which is represented by the same bar as the durability bar. Torches are quite effective last-resort melee weapons when lit, but when hitting an object or building loses durability significantly faster than most other items in Rust. Be cautious when lighting a torch outside at night, because this makes you highly visible to other players, some of which may try to kill you.

== Crafting ==
A Torch can be crafted with:
* 30 [[Wood]]
* 1 [[Cloth]]
* 1 [[Low Grade Fuel]]
It takes 30 seconds to craft a Torch.
