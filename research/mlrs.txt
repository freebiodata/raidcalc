= MLRS Rocket =
The MLRS Rocket is a projectile for the MLRS, they are found in Locked Crates as well as the MLRS Aiming Module. It has lethal damage to both structures and players. 3 SAM Sites can easily destroy the projectiles.

Damage: 500

Explosion Radius: 15 m 

Explosion Delay: 60-70 sec
